# DrishtiAssist User Manual & Field Guide

## 1. Quick Start Guide for Visually Impaired Users

1. **How to Hold the Phone:**
   - Hold your phone at chest level, either handheld or in a secure chest-strap/lanyard.
   - Point the rear camera forward toward the walking path 2 to 4 meters ahead.
   - If using headphones, we strongly recommend **open-ear bone-conduction headphones** (e.g. Shokz) so your natural hearing of street traffic, horns, and ambient cues is not blocked.

2. **Starting the App:**
   - Double-tap the giant **"Start Detection"** button (or say *"Start"* into your microphone).
   - You will hear: *"DrishtiAssist active. Monitoring walking path."*
   - The phone's screen will stay awake automatically.

3. **Understanding the Voice & Audio Alerts:**
   - **Hazard phrase:** Short, actionable direction: *"Pothole, left"* or *"Stairs, ahead"*.
   - **Stereo beep:** A high-pitch double chirp will sound in your left or right ear indicating which side the hazard is on.
   - **Caution phrase:** If lighting is low or a surface is ambiguous below 60% confidence, DrishtiAssist will say: *"Not sure, use your cane carefully"*.

4. **Hands-Free Voice Commands:**
   Say any of the following commands clearly:
   - *"Start"* / *"शुरू"*: Begin hazard scanning.
   - *"Stop"* / *"रुकें"*: Stop camera scanning.
   - *"Read"* / *"पढ़ो"*: OCR sign reading of the sign in front of you.
   - *"Describe"* / *"विवरण"*: Request a 2-sentence AI scene description.
   - *"Repeat"* / *"दोहराएं"*: Repeat the last spoken warning.
   - *"Louder"* / *"तेज़"*: Increase volume.
   - *"Quieter"* / *"धीमे"*: Decrease volume.
   - *"Hindi"* / *"हिन्दी"*: Switch to Hindi voice output.
   - *"English"* / *"अंग्रेजी"*: Switch to Indian English voice output.
   - *"Correct"* / *"Wrong"*: Report whether an alert was accurate.

## 2. White Cane Safety Protocol

> **CRITICAL RULE:** DrishtiAssist is an ASSISTIVE AID ONLY.  
> It expands your preview horizon, but it **never replaces physical ground contact** with your cane tip. Always sweep your white cane one step ahead of your feet to physically verify curbs, steps, and potholes before stepping.
